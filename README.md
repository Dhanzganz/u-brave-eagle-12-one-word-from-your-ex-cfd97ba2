Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Word From Your Ex** — one word your ex would use to describe you, judged pairwise by an AI judge on funniest + most painfully honest.

## The entry

**exhausting**

Ten letters of painful honesty. Every ex has said it, every dater has lived it, and the entrant plainly knows it. It stings because it is true — and that is exactly why it is funny.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 10 characters, well within the 1–24 character gate).
